dashboards

All of it, in one place.

If you run more than one site or app, the numbers live in five different consoles. Graphley pulls them into one window, one color per site, so you can see the whole picture before your coffee is cold.

Google Analytics, without the console

Sign in with Google once. Graphley finds every GA4 property you can read and asks which ones to track. Visitors, page views, key events and top pages come straight from Google’s Data API.

App Store downloads and ratings

Add an App Store Connect key and Graphley reads your daily sales reports: first-time downloads by app and by device, next to each app’s rating and review count.

Dashboards you build

Make a dashboard for a launch, a client or a morning check. Add line charts, bars and single numbers from any source.

Refresh on your schedule

Graphley refreshes in the morning and whenever you ask. A source that fails is skipped and flagged, and the rest still update.

iphone · coming soon

Check it from anywhere.

The iPhone app is coming soon. It will show the same numbers as your Mac: your Mac does the fetching and writes a snapshot to your private iCloud database, and your iPhone reads it.

  • Your iCloud, not ours. Data syncs through your own private CloudKit database. Graphley has no server to send it to.
  • Keys stay on the Mac. Google and App Store Connect credentials live in the Keychain and never travel with the numbers.
  • Fresh in the morning. When the Mac refreshes, the phone gets a quiet push and updates.
agents

Let your agent do the setup.

Graphley has an MCP server built in. Connect Claude Code, Codex or Claude chat and it can find your Google Analytics properties, add sites and apps, build dashboards and answer questions about your numbers. Every change it makes is logged in the app.

How agent setup works
Graphley › Settings › Agents

I just launched tidepool.app. Track it, and add it to my Launch dashboard.

  • discover_ga4_properties7 properties · “Tidepool” not tracked yet
  • add_siteTidepool · GA4 property 482913077
  • add_widgetLaunch › Visitors per day · Tidepool
  • refresh_all5 sites · 2 apps refreshed
  • add_siteAgents can view only. Turn on “Let agents make changes” in Settings → Agents.

Done. Tidepool is tracked and on your Launch dashboard. It had 148 visitors yesterday.

I found the Tidepool property. Graphley is set to view only, so I haven’t added it.

privacy

Your numbers stay yours.

No Graphley server

Graphley talks to Google and Apple directly from your Mac. There’s no account to make and nothing for us to store.

Keys in the Keychain

Sign-in tokens and App Store Connect keys are kept in the Keychain. They never go into synced data or into an agent’s hands.

Agents on your terms

The MCP server runs on your Mac. You choose whether agents can only look, or can also make changes. How agent access works

download

Graphley for Mac.

Free for Macs running macOS 14 or later. Code signed, notarized by Apple, and kept up to date automatically.

Download and install MCP setup
  1. Open the disk image and drag Graphley into Applications.
  2. Launch Graphley and sign in with Google to find your Google Analytics properties.
  3. Pick what to track, add an App Store Connect key if you have apps, and your dashboard fills in.

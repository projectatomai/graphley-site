Privacy

Graphley is made by Protomota, LLC. Your numbers stay on your devices.

The app

Graphley stores your workspace (the sites, apps and dashboards you set up) and the numbers it fetches on your Mac. It fetches them directly from Google Analytics and App Store Connect using your own sign-in and keys. Graphley has no server and no account, and sends nothing to Protomota.

Sign-in tokens and keys are kept in the Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, they are available on your other Macs.

To check for updates, Graphley downloads a small feed from graphley.ai. The request carries the app and macOS version, as any web request does, and no analytics data.

iPhone and iCloud

When the iPhone app is available, the Mac will write a snapshot of your numbers to your private iCloud database so your iPhone can show them. Only you can read it. Keys and tokens are never part of the snapshot.

Local agent connections

The MCP server runs on your Mac when an agent starts it, and works on the same local workspace. You choose whether agents can only view, or can also make changes, and whether they can save keys. Changes made by agents are listed in Settings → Agents.

Your chosen agent may send information it retrieves to its AI provider. Review that provider’s privacy terms before connecting.

Website and support

The site provides information about Graphley. Hosting providers may process request information to deliver the site. If you contact us, we use the details you send to respond.

If you click Allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits, page views and link clicks. Page addresses are sent without query strings, and advertising features are off. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load. Change your choice any time with Analytics settings in the footer.

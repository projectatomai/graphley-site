Common questions

What is Graphley?

Graphley is a free analytics app for Mac, made by Protomota. It puts every website and App Store app you run in one dashboard, using Google Analytics 4 and App Store Connect, with no Graphley server. Your AI agents can set it up through its built-in MCP server.

How do I get it?

Download the signed and notarized disk image from graphley.ai/download, open it and drag Graphley into Applications. It needs macOS 14 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. Graphley is free and source available, with no account, no trial and nothing to unlock.

How does it update?

Graphley checks for new versions and installs signed updates automatically. You can also choose Check for Updates from the Graphley menu.

Is there an iPhone app?

The iPhone app is coming soon. It will show the same numbers as your Mac, synced through your own private iCloud database.

Which sources does it read?

Google Analytics 4 for websites, App Store Connect sales reports for app downloads, App Store ratings and reviews in every storefront, Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools for search, and follower counts from X, Instagram, YouTube and GitHub (Substack and LinkedIn by hand).

Where is my data kept?

On your Mac. Graphley has no server. Sign-in tokens and keys stay in the Keychain.

Which AI agents can work with Graphley?

Claude Code, Codex and Claude chat, through a local MCP server with 23 tools. You choose whether agents can only view, or can also make changes.
